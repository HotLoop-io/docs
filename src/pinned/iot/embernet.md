HotLoop IoT is the automation base, small enough for a Raspberry Pi. EmberNet packages and deploys it from the App Store, and the EmberNet docs cover that side.

Where it is on the EmberNet docs:

- [HotLoop IoT on EmberNet](https://docs.embernet.ai/docs/app-store/hotloop-iot)
- [App Store Overview](https://docs.embernet.ai/docs/app-store/overview)
- [HotLoop Gateway on EmberNet](https://docs.embernet.ai/docs/app-store/hotloop)
- [HotLoop Edge on EmberNet](https://docs.embernet.ai/docs/app-store/hotloop-edge)
- [HotLoop Edge Relay on EmberNet](https://docs.embernet.ai/docs/app-store/hotloop-edge-relay)

To run it at a plant, sign up at [embernet.ai](https://embernet.ai/). [Using HotLoop in a business](/business/) covers EmberNet and a rollout by Fireball Industries, HotLoop's master systems integrator.
