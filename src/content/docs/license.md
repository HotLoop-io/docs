Every HotLoop product ships under the HotLoop Source License Agreement 1.0. You get the source, you can change it, and you can run it free at home, in the lab, at school, at a nonprofit or in government. Every version becomes AGPL-3.0 or later four years after it is released.

## What you can do

- **Read, copy, change and share it.** You can copy, modify and redistribute HotLoop and build your own work on it, and use it for anything that is not production use.
- **Run it at home and in the lab.** Personal, hobby, educational, evaluation, development and testing use is free.
- **Run it at a school, nonprofit or government body.** Accredited schools, nonprofits and government bodies can run HotLoop for their own operations, and can have a contractor install and operate it for them on equipment they own or control.
- **Build Spark SDK and Transmitter into what you make.** Put the Spark SDK in your own firmware and the HotLoop Transmitter library in your own PLC projects, and sell the compiled result to your customers.

## Using HotLoop in a business

Running HotLoop to support a company's operations, building it into something you sell, or hosting it for your customers is business use. Email EmberNet, HotLoop's Gold Licensee, at [info@hotloop.ai](mailto:info@hotloop.ai), tell us what you want to run and where, and we'll set you up with the right license for it. [Using HotLoop in a business](/business/) says more about what counts.

## Every version becomes AGPL

Four years after a version of a HotLoop product is first released, that version is also available under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later. From then on, anyone can use that version under either license.

## It covers the whole lineup

One agreement covers HotLoop IoT, Edge, Gateway, Edge Relay, Flow, Cascade, HotLoop for CODESYS and Transmitter, so there is one set of terms to read. A version released under an earlier license stays under that license.
