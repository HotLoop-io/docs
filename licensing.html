Licensing

Two products, two licenses. Which one applies depends on the product, and for the Gateway, on who is running it.

ProductWhoCostHow to get it
HotLoop Gateway Individual, home, hobbyist, nonprofit, or education Free Self-host directly, no agreement needed
HotLoop Gateway Any business or for-profit use, internal or customer-facing Enterprise Exclusively through Embernet
HotLoop Flow Anyone, including businesses and commercial use Free Self-host directly under Apache-2.0, no agreement needed

The two products are licensed separately on purpose. HotLoop Gateway is source-available under the HotLoop Community License, which is not an OSI-approved open source license, and which is the text further down this page. HotLoop Flow is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0, which is an OSI-approved open source license with no business-use restriction. A business does not need Embernet, or anyone else's permission, to run HotLoop Flow.

The table above is the plain language version. For the Gateway, the legal text below is what actually decides things, so read it before you assume your use case is covered. It's kept in the HotLoop-io/HotLoop-io repo.

One note on history, so nobody finds it by surprise. Container images for earlier versions of what is now HotLoop Gateway, versions 4.0.0 through 4.3.1, were published publicly under Apache-2.0, along with the Helm chart packages released beside them. They remain available, and they remain under Apache-2.0. The HotLoop Community License applies to the Gateway from its first release under the HotLoop name onward. HotLoop Flow, first published as Emberwire, has been Apache-2.0 the whole time and is not changing.
