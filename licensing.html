Licensing

HotLoop uses one license with two very different outcomes depending on who's running it.

Use caseCostHow to get it
Individual / home / hobbyist / nonprofit / education Free Self-host directly, no agreement needed
Any business / for-profit use, internal or customer-facing Enterprise Exclusively through Embernet

The full legal text is the canonical source of truth — read it before assuming your use case is covered. It's kept in the HotLoop-io/HotLoop-io repo.
